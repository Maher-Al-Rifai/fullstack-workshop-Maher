# Code of conduct

This training environment must remain professional, respectful, and safe for learning.

Participants are expected to:

- critique work and decisions rather than people;
- explain review feedback with enough context to learn from it;
- avoid humiliating demonstrations, surprise assessments, or publishing private learner mistakes;
- protect credentials, personal data, and internal information;
- give credit for contributions and ask before recording sessions;
- raise blockers early and accept that uncertainty is part of engineering.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate sabotage, credential misuse, and retaliation are not acceptable. Report concerns to the designated workshop owner or the organization's established conduct channel. The owner should document and address reports promptly while limiting disclosure to those who need to act.
